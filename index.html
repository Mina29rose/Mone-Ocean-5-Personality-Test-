Welcome!!!

This is a small game inspired by Nicky Case's The Evolution of Trust and uses the O.C.E.A.N. personality model, one of the most widely researched models of personality. O stands for Openness, which shows how curious and open you are to new ideas. C stands for Conscientiousness, which shows how organised and responsible you are. E stands for Extraversion, which shows how outgoing and energetic you are. A stands for Agreeableness, which shows how kind and cooperative you are. N stands for Neuroticism, which shows how emotionally sensitive you are to stress and challenges. Everyone has their own unique combination of these five traits, and that combination can tell us a lot about someone and what works best for them. For that reason, I took all O.C.E.A.N. 5 questions and picked the most valuable ones. After that, I turned the remaining questions into real life situations that you could experience during everyday school life. I did this so you can understand everything better, and so it doesn't feel like you're just taking another personality test. Instead, you can imagine yourself in each scenario. This test will measure whether you're Low, Low to Mid, Mid, Mid to High, or High in each O.C.E.A.N. trait. Based on your results, I'll teach you which study habits work best for you, explain why they work, and give you important personalised tips that are customised specifically to you. So remember to be honest with yourself
